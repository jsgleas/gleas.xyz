# Hybrid embroidery + DTG merch playbook

Operating guide for premium hybrid shirts under GLEAS.xyz (Shopify-first). Cookie Monkey is the first IP drop; GLEAS mark tees sit alongside. Products live in Shopify later; this repo stays the mall map, not the factory floor.

## Why hybrid embroidery + DTG

- **DTG** — full-color art (faces, gradients, complex prints) on the body.
- **Embroidery** — durable logo/text/texture accents (chest mark, sleeve, collar, hem tab).

Use each method where it wins: DTG for the big graphic, embroidery for a small brand mark or tactile accent. Do not stack both on the same surface fighting for the same area.

Longevity comes from **blank quality + wash care + stitch/print placement**, not from more decoration.

## Product formula (lock before hunting vendors)

### Blank

- Midweight or heavyweight cotton, approx **6–7 oz / 180–220 gsm**, ring-spun or combed.
- Prefer blanks known to take both DTG and embroidery. Evaluate:
  - Comfort Colors 1717
  - Los Angeles Apparel 1801 / 1820
  - Independent Trading (tees / fleece as needed)
  - Next Level (lighter drops only)
- Reject blanks that fail DTG pretreat, embroidery puckering, or pigment-dye bleed on sample.

### Decoration layout

- **Large DTG print:** back **or** left chest only (one primary surface).
- **Embroidery:** small left chest / sleeve / hem tab — max about **2–3"** so stitch density stays clean.
- Leave about **1"** clearance between DTG edge and embroidery.
- Keep embroidery off high-stretch zones and thick DTG underbase.

### Colorways and sizes

- **Colorways:** 2–3 garment colors max for the first drop (e.g. black, bone, washed green).
- **Sizes:** S–2XL standard; sample **M** and **XL** before any production run.

### Care

Ship a care card with every order:

```txt
Cold wash inside-out. Hang dry. Do not iron decoration.
```

## Art rules

- Prefer **vector** logos for embroidery; high-res **PNG/TIFF** for DTG.
- Avoid micro-detail in embroidery; convert fine type to DTG or simplify.
- One hero graphic per shirt.
- Embroidered accent = GLEAS or Cookie Monkey brand mark (not a second full illustration).

## Finding makers who can do both

POD apps that only do DTG or only embroidery usually cannot combine cleanly.

| Path | When to use |
|---|---|
| Specialty hybrid POD (e.g. Printful embroidery + DTG on the same item if supported for that blank — verify per SKU) | Low volume, test designs |
| Local / screen print shop that also embroiders | Better quality control; send blank + art files |
| Cut-and-sew / private label | Only after a design sells |

### Vendor vetting checklist

1. Confirm the **same blank** supports both DTG and embroidery (some heavy pigment-dyed tees reject one method).
2. Order a **paid sample** of the exact combo (not catalog photos).
3. Wash-test **5 cycles** (see sample protocol below).
4. Ask for stitch count, thread brand, DTG pretreat/cure process, and reprint policy.
5. Compare unit cost at **12 / 50 / 100** units so you know when to leave POD.

## Sample wash protocol

For every candidate blank + decoration combo:

1. Photograph front, back, embroidery close-up, and DTG close-up before wash.
2. Wash cold, inside-out, gentle cycle; hang dry. Repeat **5** times.
3. Fail if any of: DTG cracking/peeling, heavy fade, embroidery thread break, puckering that does not relax, color bleed onto light areas, or neck/shoulder distortion from stitch density.
4. Pass only if hand-feel and brand readability still feel premium after wash 5.
5. Keep a short note: blank SKU, vendor, stitch count, DTG size, pass/fail, and unit cost.

## Selling better (not just making better)

GLEAS.xyz is the mall; brand IP sells.

- **Positioning:** “Built to keep” — blank weight, hybrid construction, and care instructions are the product story. No feature dump.
- **Shopify (later):** products live in Shopify; gleas.xyz links out. Use product metafields for blank brand, decoration methods, and care.
- **Photos:** real garment on body + flat lay showing embroidery texture and DTG print. Texture is the proof. No mockup-only launch listings.
- **Pricing:** cost × **2.5–3.5** for premium hybrid. Do not undercut against cheap DTG-only POD.
